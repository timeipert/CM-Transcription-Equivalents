# Pattern Library

The **Patterns** page is the one place for the notation vocabulary of your project: every pattern that occurs in the transcriptions, your equivalents tables or your annotations, shown with its code, its Ref ID, example images from your annotations, and notes.

## What you see
- A **hierarchy** of patterns, grouped first by melodic direction and then by how the notes are connected, so related shapes sit together.
- Per pattern: the **code**, its **label**, its **Ref ID** from the equivalents tables, how often it occurs, and how many of your annotations show it. Open a pattern to see **image examples** cut from your manuscript annotations.
- A search box (code, label or Ref ID) and a filter *only annotated*.

## What you can do
- **Label and describe** a pattern: the label and notes are saved with your workspace.
- **Add a pattern by hand** for a shape that exists only on a scan and in no transcription. The code must contain a `*` (e.g. `*u` or `[*ud]`).
- **Create code variants**: derive a variant from a base shape with one of your [custom signs](./settings-and-data).
- **Edit the MEI template** of a pattern: the neume components used when patterns are exported as MEI.
- **Configure the variant buttons** (*snippet variants*): the classifier letters offered when you annotate a snippet — by default a–g, or your own list.

Everything on this page is saved in the workspace and travels with your backups.
